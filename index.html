Frequently asked questions

Does Fuji install the equipment?

No. FujiLatam manufactures and exports. Installation and aftersales stay with the installation company in each country. That is why we work through distributors and do not compete with our own customers.

Do you sell to developers directly?

No. If a developer contacts us we send them to the distributor in their country. Where a country has no distributor yet, we serve them from the factory until the territory is appointed.

Why do you not publish prices?

Because almost everything is decided by sealed tender. Publishing a range hands competitors a reference for where our price sits, and it anchors the client on a figure that almost never matches their building.

What speed does my building need?

The standard is 1 m/s and on most projects that is enough. A six or ten stop building never reaches 2 m/s, and going down you never reach nominal speed. Before paying for speed it is worth looking at door opening, closing and the acceleration ramp, which is where it is actually felt.

Do I need training to install Fuji equipment?

Not in order to buy. If you already run an installation company you already have your own method. Training at the main factory in China and on site training are additional services, and many distributors use them for new equipment.

What if my country already has a distributor?

We pass your contact to the distributor that already represents Fuji there. If you still want to work with us, write to us: every territory is handled differently and we look at it case by case.

How fast do you reply?

Immediately. In this business email gets answered the same day.

Do you build outside the catalogue?

Yes. We build to measure in dimensions and in design. If the shaft already exists, we design the equipment that fits that shaft.
