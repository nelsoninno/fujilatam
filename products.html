Product lines

Elevators

Passenger, home, panoramic, hospital, freight and hydraulic. It is the highest volume line and the one that shapes the rest of the project.

Machine roomless passenger elevator

The workhorse. With no machine room it wins back the space others lose in the overhead slab. It is the default choice in residential and offices.

Capacity: 450 - 2000 kg (6 - 26 pers.)
Speed: 1.0 - 2.5 m/s

Small machine room passenger elevator

When the building needs more speed or more travel. It reaches 4 m/s and takes duplex and triplex configuration for peak hours.

Capacity: 450 - 2000 kg (6 - 26 pers.)
Speed: 1.0 - 4.0 m/s

Panoramic elevator

Semicircle, square or cylindrical capsule. For atriums, shopping malls and buildings where the equipment is part of the design.

Capacity: 450 - 2000 kg (6 - 26 pers.)
Speed: 1.0 - 4.0 m/s

Home lift

Three to five people, 0.4 m/s, 800 mm pit. For houses and villas with no built shaft.

Capacity: 260 - 400 kg (3 - 5 pers.)
Speed: 0.4 m/s

Residential hydraulic platform

For homes, wheelchairs and older people. 2 to 6 stops and up to 15 m of travel, indoors or outdoors. It is hydraulic and slow on purpose: it is not a traction elevator.

Capacity: 150 - 400 kg

Hospital elevator

A long car sized for a wheeled stretcher. It is what defines the shaft in a hospital or clinic, and the one most often forgotten in design.

Capacity: 1600 - 2000 kg (21 - 26 pers.)
Speed: 1.0 - 2.5 m/s

Freight elevator

630 kg to 10,000 kg, geared, gearless, machine roomless or explosion proof. In hotels and airports it is not optional.

Capacity: 630 - 10 000 kg
Speed: 0.25 - 4.0 m/s

Hydraulic elevator

Piston, pump and oil. Slower and cheaper, useful on few levels and short travel.

Capacity: 1000 - 5000 kg
Speed: 0.25 - 0.5 m/s

Dumbwaiter

100 to 300 kg, vertical opening. Kitchens, hotels, laboratories and pharmacies.

Capacity: 100 - 300 kg
Speed: 0.4 - 1.0 m/s

Home elevator

Four traction systems for homes

Motor on top and no machine room. Faster and with more load than a hydraulic platform. The choice depends on the shaft and the structure of the house.

Traction

The classic design. Runs on 220 V single phase and draws 1.5 kW, less than a microwave oven.

Without counterweight

Rope suspension with no counterweight. Uses up to 30% more of the shaft area, for homes with little space.

Backpack frame

For narrow, deep shafts. Allows doors at 90 degrees and openings on three sides.

Steel belt

A composite belt instead of wire rope: 20% lighter, no lubrication and 2 to 3 times the service life.

Car elevator

Capacity: 3000 - 5000 kg

Mechanical parking

From two post lifts to rotary towers and fully automated systems. Doubles or triples parking without buying more land.

Capacity: 2 - 15
